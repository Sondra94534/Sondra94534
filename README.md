# Hi, I’m Sondra Hoffman 👋

I’m an independent technology consultant specializing in **business intelligence, information systems, workflow analysis, and responsible AI adoption**.

My work explores how organizations can use data and technology to make better decisions—without losing sight of the people affected by those decisions. I am especially interested in the point where **cutting-edge technology and genuine human empathy come together**.

## What I’m working on

* Developing practical tools for evaluating and improving business workflows
* Exploring responsible, human-centered applications of artificial intelligence
* Helping small and service-based organizations make informed technology decisions
* Creating business intelligence, data governance, and systems-analysis resources
* Building the **Measured Change Toolkit**, a guided framework for testing workflow changes before making larger technology investments

## Areas of interest
